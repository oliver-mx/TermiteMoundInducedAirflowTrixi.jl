TermiteMoundInducedAirflowTrixi

Documentation for TermiteMoundInducedAirflowTrixi.jl.

TermiteMoundInducedAirflowTrixi.jl is a numerical simulation package written in Julia. It solves a thermo-fluid-dynamic model of the airflow inside a termite mound with the discontinuous Galerkin method. The package builds on the numerical simulation framework for conservation laws Trixi.jl.

The termite mound model (TME)

The 1D termite mound model (termite mound equations, TME) is a thermo-fluid-dynamic model of the airflow through the flow channel of a termite mound. The channel is a closed loop: it runs from the soil up through the flute to the top of the mound, down through the chimney and back through the soil. It is parametrized by the scaled arc length $x \in [0, 1]$ with periodic boundary conditions. The model is a low Mach number limit of the Euler equations of gas dynamics (see Mathematics). All quantities are scaled.

\[\begin{aligned} \frac{\partial \rho}{\partial t} + \frac{\partial (\rho u)}{\partial x} &= -\frac{A_x}{A} \rho u, \\ \frac{\partial T_i}{\partial t} &= k_i (T - T_i), \\ \frac{\partial p_0}{\partial t} + \gamma p_0 \frac{\partial u}{\partial x} &= -\gamma \frac{A_x}{A} u p_0 - \frac{k_w}{\sqrt{A}} (T - T_u), \\ \frac{\mathrm{d} v}{\mathrm{d} t} &= \frac{1}{\int_0^1 \frac{\rho}{A} \, dy} \int_0^1 \Big[ -\rho u u_x - \frac{\beta}{A} \rho u \big(\eta + (1-\eta)|u|\big) \\ &\hphantom{= \frac{1}{\int_0^1 \frac{\rho}{A} \, dy} \int_0^1 \Big[} - \frac{h_x}{Fr^2} (\rho - \rho_{h_0}) \Big] \, dy, \\ p_0 &= \rho T, \\ u(t, x) &= \frac{v(t)}{A(x)} + \frac{1}{A(x)} \int_0^x \frac{A(y)}{\gamma p_0} \left[ -\frac{\mathrm{d} p_0}{\mathrm{d} t} - \frac{k_w}{\sqrt{A}} (T - T_u) \right] dy. \end{aligned}\]

Unknowns:

  • air density $\rho(t, x)$ and air velocity $u(t, x)$,
  • leading-order pressure $p_0(t)$ (constant in space) and air temperature $T = p_0/\rho$,
  • mound material temperature $T_i(t, x)$,
  • auxiliary velocity $v(t) = A(0) \, u(t, 0)$, the volume flux at $x = 0$.

Model data:

  • cross section $A(x)$ of the flow channel,
  • height profile $h(x)$ of the flow channel (its slope $h_x$ drives the buoyancy),
  • boundary (wall) temperature $T_u(t, x, T_i)$: the soil temperature in the soil, the mean of the ambient air temperature and $T_i$ in the flute, and $T_i$ in the chimney.

The last equation is the velocity constraint. Since $p_0$ does not depend on $x$, the $p_0$ equation does not evolve a field. Instead, it determines the velocity $u$. The model is therefore a system of a transport PDE for $\rho$, ODEs for $v$ and $p_0$, an ODE for $T_i$ at every point $x$ ($T_i$ is not transported) and an algebraic equation for $u$.

What the package provides

  • The equations TermiteMoundEquations1D for Trixi.jl, a high-performance framework for conservation laws with the discontinuous Galerkin (DG) method, together with the model parameters (termite_parameters) and the model data (cross section, height profile and boundary temperature) of termite mounds.
  • Two numerical methods for the PDE-ODE system with algebraic constraint:
    • a coupled approach (CoupledSourceTerms + AlgebraicVelocityCallback): all equations are integrated together. This is the recommended approach.
    • a Godunov-type splitting (GodunovSplittingCallback): a first-order operator splitting.
  • A manufactured solution to verify both methods (see Manufactured solution).
  • Example elixirs in examples/ for a termite mound, a reference mound and the manufactured solution, each for both methods.

Installation

If you have not yet installed Julia, please follow the instructions for your operating system. TermiteMoundInducedAirflowTrixi.jl works with Julia v1.10 and newer. We recommend using the latest stable release of Julia.

To run the examples, TermiteMoundInducedAirflowTrixi.jl, Trixi.jl (DG solver framework), OrdinaryDiffEqLowStorageRK.jl (time integration) and Plots.jl (visualization) can be added and installed by executing the following commands in the Julia REPL:

using Pkg
Pkg.add(["TermiteMoundInducedAirflowTrixi", "Trixi", "OrdinaryDiffEqLowStorageRK", "Plots"])
